How K-Name & K-Match works

K-Name & K-Match is a free, fan-made tool for people who love Korean music, dramas and culture. Pick a vibe and get a Korean name with Hangul, romanization, Hanja meanings and a playful idol-style profile. Or enter two birthdays to see a K-style compatibility score with a ship name, a drama title and four chemistry bars.

  • Results are generated in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded.
  • Save a result card for Instagram (feed or story) or share a link on Facebook, WhatsApp, LINE, Telegram and X.
  • Hear how your Korean name sounds with the Listen button.
  • Available in English, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Chinese, French and Spanish.
